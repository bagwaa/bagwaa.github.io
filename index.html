Profile

I'm a Senior Laravel Developer based in Nottingham who has been building for the web since 1996. I'm a programmer at heart and spend a lot of my own time staying current with the rapidly evolving landscape of web development.

My core expertise lies in PHP, Laravel, and JavaScript. I've been working with Laravel since version 3 and have grown with the framework through every major release. Alongside this, I have extensive experience with a range of JavaScript libraries and frameworks, including Vue, React, and earlier in my career, jQuery. I also enjoy exploring other programming languages outside of my day job, such as Rust and Go.

Writing clean, understandable, and maintainable code is central to how I work. I lean heavily on automated testing, code reviews, and feedback loops, while balancing technical excellence with the business reality that the pursuit of perfection shouldn't come at an unsustainable cost.

More recently, AI-assisted development has become a core part of how I work. I use LLMs like Claude, Codex, and Gemini in my day-to-day coding, but always in a responsible and professional way. Strong automated test coverage, careful prompting, and thorough human review are the guardrails that keep the output trustworthy and let me move quickly without ever trading away the quality of the work.
